Theorem 13. effect preserves the ⋄ operation. That is, ∀𝑓, 𝑔 ∈ 𝔈Γ:
effectΓ(𝑓) ⋄ effectΓ(𝑔) = effectΓ(𝑓 ⋄ 𝑔) (15)

and pr1 ∘ (𝑓 ⋄ 𝑔) = (pr1 ∘ 𝑓) ∘ (pr1 ∘ 𝑔). Then
(effectΓ(𝑓) ⋄ effectΓ(𝑔))(𝛾, 𝜑) = ((𝜀, 𝜑 ∘ 𝑠 ∘ 𝑡), trackΓ(𝑠, pr1 ∘ 𝑔) ∘ trackΓ(𝑡, pr1 ∘ 𝑓))
