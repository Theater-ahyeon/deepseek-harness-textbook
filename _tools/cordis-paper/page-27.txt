monoid. That is,
1. if every generator of 𝔐(𝑒1) commutes with every generator of 𝔐(𝑒2), then every element

with 𝑓 likewise form a submonoid containing the generators of 𝔐(𝑒2) and hence 𝔐(𝑒2).
