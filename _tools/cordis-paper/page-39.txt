4.3.  Metatheory
This section establishes the metatheory of the calculus: that every rule preserves the well-
formedness of the registry ( Section  4.3.1); that temporal and spatial composability hold in 
their global form, one fiber’s guarantee surviving whatever the other fibers do in between 
(Section 4.3.2, Section 4.3.3); that the system quiesces (Section 4.3.4); and that it quiesces where 
a load of the same configuration from scratch would have left it (Section 4.3.5).
