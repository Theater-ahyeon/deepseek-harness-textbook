4.2.  The Calculus
This section gives the calculus: nine rules generating two relations. An orchestration rule, 
prefixed O- and written 𝛾 ⇒ 𝛿, is an action the orchestrator may perform; its premises say when 
the action is legal, not when it occurs. A lifecycle rule, prefixed L- and written 𝛾 ⟶ 𝛿, is a step 
the system takes unprompted whenever its premises hold. A sequence of steps interleaves the
