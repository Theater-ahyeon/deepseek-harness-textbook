discipline of O-Insert the two drive the same transitions, a key having one possible provider 
there. The view supplies the vocabulary of the results above, Theorem 70 and Theorem 71 both

different realms and the provisions no longer force the view. The implementation carries that 
scoping and holds the view in fiber.committed (Section 5.1.3).
